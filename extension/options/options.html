Welcome to SilhouetteAI

SilhouetteAI detects personal information in your AI chat prompts and redacts it before submission. All detection runs locally — nothing is sent anywhere.

By default, the extension is active on ChatGPT. Try it: type a prompt that contains an email or phone number, then press Enter.
